## What changed and why

- Shared organization approval, team ownership and pending-event rules close sibling-route bypasses and inconsistent owner access. Game/Event writes are atomic and use the correct lifecycle enum.
- Ad purchase references and overlap checks protect delivery and refund ownership across fan, coach and organization-owner purchases. The read-only production preview found no affected records among three completed purchases.
- Host-request hydration and submission handling stop inconsistent rendering and false failure after successful creation.
- Feedback is persisted before acknowledgment, with reporter-scoped retry deduplication and observable notification failure.
- OTA/runtime/deployment tags improve incident attribution; health alerts execute on failure; Snyk distinguishes scan failure from findings and avoids committing raw scanner output.
- Matrix reports and the nightly triage procedure retain repros, version evidence and unresolved acceptance instead of treating old notes as current certification.

This release also carries the prior September 5 work already in its ancestry: owner-note/UI and privacy fixes, guarded production exports/startup/checkout repair, resilient session restoration, and private data-export lifecycle/content safeguards. Their dated evidence remains in the [readiness plan](../../release/A_PLUS_READINESS_PLAN_2026-09-05.md); they are not counted as newly implemented again.

## A+ assessment

A+ is not earned yet. The reproduced backend and settings defects have tested fixes and release delivery evidence. A numeric completion percentage would conceal those unknowns.
